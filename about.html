About this project

About Us

Appliance Energy Consumption Website is a student project by Tin Tran for COS30045 Data Visualisation. It brings together a three-page website and television charts built from course-provided data.

What the site shows

The Televisions page explores model counts, screen technology, size groups, star ratings and labelled annual energy consumption. An electricity spot-price chart provides a separate time-series example. The figures describe records in the supplied files, not Australian sales or household electricity bills.

How to use it

Use the navigation at the top of every page. On Home, select a FAQ question to open or close its answer. On Televisions, use the screen-technology buttons above the histogram and hover over a point in the scatterplot for model details.

Project scope

Home contains clearly marked placeholder copy for future Australian appliance guidance. The chart data and their limits are described beside the visualisations. The README records the data files and the role of generative AI in development.
