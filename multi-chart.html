1. Vertical bar chart

For 55-inch TVs, how does average labelled annual electricity consumption vary by screen technology?

Source: supplied cleaned TV data; 55-inch records grouped by technology. This compares registered models, not sales.

2. Scatter and line chart

How did the average electricity spot price change from 1998 to 2024? Dots show yearly observations; the line connects them in time order.

Source: course file ARE_Spot_Prices.csv; its average column excludes Tasmania and Snowy, as named in the data.

3. Donut chart

What share of the TV model records fall into small, medium, and large screen-size groups?

Size groups: small up to 43 inches; medium 44-65 inches; large 66 inches and above. The Week 2 exercise wording does not place 43 and 66 inches clearly, so these explicit boundaries include every model once.
